# Geothermal system — Walker Lane, alteration halos, and why faults are missing

**Synthesis sources:** Faulds et al. (DOI 10.1016/j.geothermics.2015.02.002), Faulds & Hinz (GES), Oskin et al. Walker Lane transtension (10.1029/2011TC002914), Wesnousky QFFD bias.

Walker Lane: 100km wide dextral shear between Sierra Nevada and Basin-Range. Faults trend ~N35W, with NE conjugate. Slip 0.5-2mm/yr. Quaternary alluvium buries scarps in 10s-100s km2 basins (Dixie Valley, Gabbs, Fairview). INGENIOUS drilled blind systems where MT + gravity + seismicity coincident but no surface scarp.

Alteration halo: 10-100m K-depletion (illite→smectite) → Th/K 2-4× background. Conductive clay 1-10 ohm-m vs host 100 ohm-m (MT cond_surf). Detrended elevation: fault-bounded basins have 50-200m hanging-wall subsidence over 2-5km half-width — visible at 1km σ not 100m.

This is why H72 targets broad divergence + Th/K, not 1m scarp.

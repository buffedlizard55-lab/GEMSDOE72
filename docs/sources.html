Data-access note

The competition training features/labels are login-walled on DrivenData and cannot be downloaded from this sandbox (egress reaches github.com and the page fetcher only; Dropbox mirrors are blocked). They must be placed in data/ from an unrestricted machine (see scripts/download_competition_data.sh) before the training pipeline can run.

04

Paleogeothermal evidence conditioned on structural alignment

Layers: GDR paleogeothermal deposits such as sinter and tufa, plus independently derived geophysical lineaments. Signature: collinear alignment of multiple documented deposits, not a radial proximity halo. Why potentially missing: fossil fluid pathways can point to structures not in the fault catalogue. Mimics: drainage-controlled carbonate deposition and clustered field documentation. Difference: line alignment versus a broad spatial prior; related paleothermal work has already been proposed in sibling research. Cost: low-medium; inspect geometry, attributes, and footprint.
